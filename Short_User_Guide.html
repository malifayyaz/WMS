WMS Quick Reference Guide

This is a short, one-page guide covering the essential daily tasks for the Wire Manufacturing Management System.

1. Dashboard (Home Screen)

Your daily business overview.

  • Summary Cards: Shows total sales, current cash/bank balances, and outstanding receivables at a glance.
  • Charts: Visualizes your profit, revenue, and expenses over the last week or month.
  • Low Stock Alerts: Instantly see if you are running low on Shiplet or Patri coil so you can order more.

2. Daily Book (Cash & Bank)

The central hub for all money coming in and going out.

  • To record money received: Click Add Receipt. Select who paid (Customer, etc.), the amount, and choose Cash or Bank. This automatically reduces their pending balance.
  • To record money spent: Click Add Payment. Select who you paid (Supplier, Expense, Worker), the amount, and choose Cash or Bank.
  • End of Day: Check the "Closing Balance" at the top. This number must exactly match the physical cash in your drawer.

3. Customers & Orders (Sales)

Tracking your finished wire sales.

  • New Order: Go to Orders > New Order. Select the customer, wire number, weight, and price.
  • The Bhatti Rule: When an order comes out of the Bhatti (furnace) and is marked "Done", you must update the final weight. The system will automatically recalculate the customer's final bill based on this new weight.
  • Payments: You can record their payments directly in their Customer Ledger or in the Daily Book.

4. Raw Materials & Suppliers (Purchases)

Tracking your Shiplet and Patri coil.

  • Receiving Coil: Go to Raw Materials > Receive Stock. Enter the supplier, coil type, weight, and price. This updates your factory stock and increases the amount you owe the supplier.
  • Quick Bhatti: Check "Send directly to Annealing" if the truck unloads the coil straight to the furnace.

5. Annealing / Bhatti

Heating the wire.

  • Send to Bhatti: Create an "Annealing Send" record to track which worker took how much weight.
  • Return from Bhatti: When the wire is ready, record it coming back. Always enter the exact returned weight so the system can calculate the burning loss.

6. Job Work (Processing Customers)

Customers who bring their own coil for you to process.

  • Receiving: Log a "Job Work Receipt". This keeps their material separate from your factory's stock.
  • Delivery & Excess: Deliver the processed wire to them. If you deliver more weight than they originally gave you, the system will warn you and deduct the extra weight from your own factory stock.

7. Expenses & Workers

Running costs and salaries.

  • Factory vs Self: Keep factory running costs (electricity, labour) separate from personal withdrawals (Fayyaz & Faisal).
  • Worker Advances: If you give a worker cash mid-week, record an Advance. The system will automatically remind you to deduct it from their salary at the end of the week.

8. AI Assistant

The fastest way to get answers.

  • Click the chat icon at the top of the screen.
  • You can ask simple questions in English or Urdu: "Ali Traders ka balance kya hai?" or "Record 5000 cash payment to Usman".
  • The AI will always show you a preview before saving anything. Click Confirm to save.

Need to fix a mistake? Almost every entry has a small pencil icon (Edit) or a red trash can (Delete) next to it. Just edit the entry and save; the system will automatically fix all related balances.
